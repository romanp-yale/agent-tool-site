Agent Tool

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

Agent Tool is a single-user assistant built and operated by Roman Parker. It runs locally on his machine and connects to his own Google account so he can ask it about his mail and calendar, and have it draft documents, in conversation or by voice.

It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to anyone else. There is no sign-up, no hosted backend, and no other user. It is published here only because Google requires an application using OAuth to have a public home page and privacy policy.

What it does

  • Answers questions in text or speech, using local speech recognition and synthesis.
  • Searches the web and reads pages to answer questions it cannot answer from memory.
  • Runs code in an isolated container for calculation and data work.
  • Reads and sends mail, reads and edits the calendar, and creates documents — all within its owner's own Google account.
  • Remembers facts its owner tells it, stored as files on his own machine.

Who can use it

One person: its owner. The application is not distributed, and no other Google account can be connected to it.
